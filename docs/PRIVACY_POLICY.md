# Train Near Me: privacy policy

Draft, 4 October 2026. Before publishing, add a contact address and host this
page at a public URL for the Play listing.

## What the app does with your location

Train Near Me uses your location for one purpose: to notice when you are near a
Mumbai suburban railway station and show you the next trains from it.

- The app asks Android to tell it when you come within your chosen distance of a
  station. Android does this check on your phone. This works when the app is
  closed, which is why the app asks for location access "all the time".
- When you open the app, it uses your current location once to find the nearest
  station.
- If you turn on high accuracy mode, the app reads your location continuously
  while that mode is on, and shows a permanent notification while it does.

**Your location never leaves your phone.** The app does not send your
coordinates to us or to anyone else, and it does not keep a history of where you
have been.

## What is sent over the internet

When the app needs a station's trains, it sends the station's code (for example
`DR` for Dadar) to our server, which fetches the trains from RailRadar
(railradar.in). Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to the
server. The request contains no account, no device identifier and no
coordinates. Our server does not store IP addresses or log individual requests;
it keeps only a count of requests per month.

## What is stored on your phone

- Your settings.
- Timetables of stations you have been near, so the app works without a
  connection.
- For each station, whether you are currently near it and when you were last
  alerted, so that you are not alerted twice. This is replaced each time and is
  not a history.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The app does not back this data up.

## What the app does not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no sharing or
selling of data.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Location, all the time | To notice a station nearby while the app is closed |
| Notifications | To show the trains |
| Run at startup | To set station alerts up again after the phone restarts |
| Foreground service | Only for high accuracy mode |

You can turn alerts off in the app, or withdraw any permission in Android
settings, at any time. Without background location the app still shows trains
when you open it.
